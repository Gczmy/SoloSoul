# Workspace and Custom Pages

The workspace is your primary interface for managing and browsing objects. SoloSoul offers both section workspaces and custom pages.

## Section Workspaces

Four fixed sections: Identity, Travel, Financial, Professional.

- Click a sidebar icon or home card to enter the corresponding section
- The workspace displays all objects of that type
- Use the top search box to filter by name

## Custom Pages

In addition to fixed sections, you can create your own pages to organize objects:

1. Click the **+** button at the bottom of the sidebar
2. Enter a page name and choose an icon
3. After creation, the page appears below the fixed sections in the sidebar
4. Objects created within that page are associated with it

## Managing Custom Pages

- **Rename**: Double-click the custom page name in the sidebar
- **Delete**: Enter the page workspace and click the **Delete** button in the top-right

<!--WARNING-->
Deleting a custom page does **not** delete the objects within it. The objects remain in their respective sections.
<!--/WARNING-->
