# Getting Started

Welcome to SoloSoul — your local-first personal digital twin engine.

## Step 1: Create an Account

1. On first launch, click **Create Account**
2. Set your **master password** (at least 12 characters recommended, with mixed case and digits)
3. (Optional) Set a password hint to aid your memory
4. Click **Create Vault** and wait for initialization

<!--WARNING-->
Your master password is the only key to your encrypted vault. It **cannot be reset**. Please memorize it or store it securely.
<!--/WARNING-->

## Step 2: Create Your First Object

1. On the home page, select the **Identity** section
2. Click the **+ Create** button
3. Choose a template, e.g., **Identity**
4. Fill in fields such as name, date of birth, nationality
5. Click **Save**

<!--STEPPER Create an identity object-->
1. Go to Home → click the **Identity** card
2. Click **+ Create** in the top-right
3. Select the **Identity** template
4. Fill in the fields
5. Click **Save**
<!--/STEPPER-->

## Step 3: Understand the Workspace

SoloSoul organizes your data into **objects**, grouped into four sections:

| Section | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| Identity | Personal info, documents |
| Travel | Passports, visas, itineraries |
| Financial | Bank accounts, cards |
| Professional | Education, work experience |

You can also create **custom pages** to organize objects your own way.

## Step 4: Protect Your Data

- Sensitive fields (e.g., ID numbers, card numbers) are automatically **blurred**
- Click a blurred field to reveal it temporarily (high-sensitivity fields may require password verification)
- Click **Lock Vault** in the sidebar when stepping away

<!--INFO-->
SoloSoul uses a zero-knowledge architecture: all data is stored locally. Developers and servers **cannot access** any of your information.
<!--/INFO-->
