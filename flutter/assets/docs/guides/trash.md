# Trash

---

## What is Soft Delete?

When you delete a chat session, SoloSoul performs a **soft delete** — the session is moved to the Trash instead of being permanently removed immediately. This provides a safety window to recover accidentally deleted sessions.

---

## Restoring a Session

1. Scroll to the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Expand the **Trash** section.
3. Find the session you want to restore.
4. Tap the **Restore** button on the right side.

> **Tip**: Restored sessions return to the main list with their original update time preserved. No data is lost.

---

## Permanently Deleting a Session

1. In the Trash section, find the target session.
2. Tap the **Delete Forever** button on the right side.
3. A confirmation dialog appears — tap **Delete Forever** to confirm.

> **Warning**: This action cannot be undone. The session and all its messages are permanently removed.

---

## Emptying the Trash

1. Expand the Trash section in the sidebar.
2. Tap the **Empty Trash** icon in the Trash header.
3. Confirm the action in the dialog.

> **Warning**: This permanently deletes all sessions in the Trash at once. There is no way to recover them.

---

## Auto-Cleanup

Sessions in Trash are automatically and permanently deleted after **30 days**. You do not need to manually empty the Trash unless you want to free up space immediately.

| Status | Location | Recoverable | Automatic Cleanup |
|--------|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| Active | Main list | N/A | N/A |
| Soft-deleted | Trash | Yes | After 30 days |
| Permanently deleted | Removed | No | Immediate |

---

## Viewing Deleted Session Details

Before deciding whether to restore or permanently delete a session, you can review its contents:

1. In the Trash section, find the target session.
2. Tap the **Details** button on the right side.
3. A dialog opens showing the full message history of that session.

> **Tip**: Use the Details view to confirm you no longer need the conversation before permanently deleting it.
